# Jira backlog request template

Use this template only when the project has no required format of its own.

```markdown
# <Sprint or feature> Jira request

## Epic

**Name or key:** <existing Epic name or key>

## Sprint goal

<One observable outcome.>

## Grooming summary

| Order | Story | Points | Scheduling |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | <Story name> | <1, 2, 3, 5, or 8> | Core |

## Story 1: <short heading>

**Epic:** <exact Epic name or key>

**Name:** <verb-led outcome>

**Description:** <why this is needed and who benefits>

**Definition of Done:** <evidence required to close the Story>

**Dependencies:** <Stories, roles, access, artifacts, or None>

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- <observable successful result>
- <observable failure, security, or recovery result when relevant>
- <repeatability or idempotency result when relevant>

**Assignee:** Unassigned. Assign a <role> during grooming.

**Story Points:** <1, 2, 3, 5, or 8>

## Deferred work

- <work intentionally excluded from the recommended sprint>
```
